# Junteng Liu

First-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present)**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.
  - Ph.D. candidate at HKUST NLP Group.
  - Ph.D. supervisor: Professor Junxian He.
  - Professor Junxian He is a Professor at HKUST NLP Group, leads HKUST NLP Group, and previously advised Junteng Liu during his undergraduate studies at SJTU.
- **B.Eng. (2020-2024)**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Junteng Liu graduated in June 2024.
- HKUST NLP Group is a research group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, led by Professor Junxian He, and focuses on natural language processing research.

## Research Experience

- **Research Intern, MINIMAX** (February 2025 - Present)
  - MINIMAX is an AI/technology company.
- **Research Intern, Tencent WXG** (June 2024 - September 2024)
  - Tencent WXG is a division of Tencent.
  - Internship advisor: Zifei Shan, who works at Tencent WXG and was Advisor to Junteng Liu during the internship at Tencent WXG.
- **Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab** (June 2023 - December 2023)
  - Shanghai AI Lab is a research institution.
  - Internship advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng, Professor associated with Shanghai AI Lab and Advisor to Junteng Liu during the internship at Shanghai AI Lab.

## Skills and Research Interests

His research approach combines natural language processing and machine learning. His research skills, focus areas, and interests are:

- Natural Language Processing
- Machine Learning
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Honors and Awards

- **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**, awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
